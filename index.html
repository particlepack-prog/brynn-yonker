Hi, I'm Brynn.

I've been obsessed with sound for as long as I can remember. It started with making movies with my siblings after school, and it never stopped.

I'm a Nashville-based sound mixer and audio engineer who loves being on set, in the field, or tucked into a quiet studio, wherever the work takes me. I studied audio engineering at Belmont University, and since graduating I've had the chance to work on everything from indie features to network television to nature documentaries.

Outside of production work, I run Sound Scout, a field recording project where I go out and capture the sounds of the natural world. It started as a passion project and has grown into something I'm really proud of, including a feature by NOAA.

I care a lot about doing good work, showing up prepared, and making sure every project sounds the way it deserves to. If that sounds like what you need, I'd love to hear about your project.
