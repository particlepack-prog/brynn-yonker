Audiobook Engineering

Recording, editing, and proofing with ACX-compliant delivery for Audible and beyond: over 120 titles and counting, from literary fiction, memoir and history to romance, thrillers and Marvel.

Every title is proofed against the manuscript and mastered to ACX loudness and noise-floor specs, so it passes review the first time. See the delivery spec →

  • Noise floor−60 dB RMS or lower
