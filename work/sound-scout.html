Sound Scout

A nature and environmental field recording project — capturing the sounds of the natural world, featured by NOAA.

Production Sound Sound Design Audiobooks Sound Scout

I go out with professional gear and capture the sounds of the natural world, often waiting a long time for the right few seconds. It started as pure love for the outdoors and has grown into something really special.

It's named after my first dog, Scout. Everything here is original: no stock footage, no library audio.
