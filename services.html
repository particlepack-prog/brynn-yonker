Services & Expertise

Five ways to work together, from a single interview day to a full audiobook. Every job starts the same way: a conversation about what the project needs to sound like, and what will get in the way of that on the day.

On set

Production Sound Mixing

Clean, usable dialogue is the one thing post can't fix later. The job on set is to capture it right the first time, so the edit is spent on the story instead of on repair.

I mix and record production audio for feature films, documentaries, network television and commercials. That means planning the setup before the shoot, placing and managing wireless and boom, monitoring every take, and flagging problems such as generators, HVAC, traffic and wardrobe rustle while there's still time to solve them.

What's included

  • Pre-production call to plan locations, talent count and mic strategy
  • Multitrack recording with isolated tracks for every mic
  • Wireless lav and boom coverage, coordinated with camera
  • Timecode sync with camera for a fast edit
  • Room tone and wild lines captured on location
  • Sound reports and organized, labeled files at wrap

Credits include work for Good Morning America, ABC 20/20, Lifetime and Outside TV. See the production work →

On set

Boom Operation

A good boom op is invisible: the mic is always where it needs to be and never in the frame.

Booming is about reading the scene. That means knowing the blocking, watching the frame lines, anticipating who speaks next and keeping the mic just outside the shot as it changes. I work alongside the mixer and camera team to keep dialogue natural and consistent across setups.

What's included

  • Boom coverage for dialogue-driven scenes
  • Coordination with camera on framing and shadows
  • Support for a separate production mixer, or as part of a full sound package
Post-production

Sound Design

Sound design is everything you feel before you notice it: the space a scene lives in, the weight of an impact, the quiet before a line lands.

I design and edit sound for commercials, short films and branded content. That covers building ambiences, layering effects, cleaning and balancing dialogue, and shaping a mix that holds up on a phone speaker as well as in a theater.

What's included

  • Dialogue cleanup and editing
  • Ambience and room tone building
  • Sound effects design and layering
  • Final mix and deliverables to your platform's spec

Studio

Audiobook Recording & Editing

A listener spends ten hours alone with a narrator's voice. Every breath, click and plosive is right in their ear, so the edit has to be meticulous.

I record, edit, proof and master audiobooks for delivery to Audible and other retailers. Proofing means following along with the manuscript to catch misreads and skipped lines, so mistakes are fixed before the book ever reaches a listener.

Delivered to ACX specification

  • Loudness−23 to −18 dB RMS
  • Peak level−3 dB maximum
  • Noise floor−60 dB RMS or lower
  • Format192 kbps CBR MP3, 44.1 kHz
  • Room tone0.5 to 1 s head, 1 to 5 s tail

Outdoors

Field Recording

Some sounds only exist in one place, at one time of day, for a few seconds.

Through my Sound Scout project I record nature and environmental audio, including birds, water and wildlife, often waiting a long time for the right moment. That work has been featured by NOAA. I also take on custom field recording for productions that need authentic location sound or ambience libraries.

What's included

  • Custom location and nature recording
  • Ambience beds for film, games and installations
  • Edited, labeled and metadata-tagged files
